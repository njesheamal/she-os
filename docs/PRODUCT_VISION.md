SHÉ OS

What is SHÉ OS?

SHÉ OS is the operating system for SHÉ ESTATE.

It is designed to unify inventory, sourcing intelligence, supplier relationships, logistics, product development, and business decision making into a single interconnected platform.

SHÉ OS is not intended to be a generic ERP. It is designed around the real workflows and philosophy of SHÉ ESTATE.

⸻

Core Philosophy

Nature organizes itself through relationships.

SHÉ ESTATE is the Sun.

Its brands are planets that orbit around that center.

New brands can form over time without requiring the system to be redesigned.

Knowledge should compound.

A sourcing trip should become permanent business intelligence.

A supplier should not just be a contact but part of a living ecosystem.

Inventory should connect to products, collections, suppliers, decisions, and sourcing history.

⸻

Design Principles

* Beauty is functionality.
* Nature over corporate.
* Relationships over lists.
* Usefulness over showmanship.
* Knowledge compounds.
* Visual hierarchy over data dumps.
* Every object belongs to a larger ecosystem.

Long Term Vision

The long term goal is to create a command center for SHÉ ESTATE that can answer questions such as:

* What deserves my attention today?
* Which suppliers are connected to this product?
* Why was this sourcing decision made?
* What did I learn during a previous trip?
* Which projects are blocked?
* How are the brands progressing?
* What do I have in inventory?
* What do I need to order to keep production efficient?
* Where should I source next?
* Which supplier is the best for a particular product?

SHÉ OS should become the institutional memory of SHÉ ESTATE.
